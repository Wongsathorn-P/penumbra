Penumbra

The Vale Observatory has been dark for eleven years. The dome is open to the storm, the floor is under two inches of black rainwater, and the breaker on the plinth is missing six fuse cells.

Find them. Every cell you seat brings another bank of lights back — and the room you have been feeling your way through turns out to look nothing like you thought.

You are not the only thing moving in here. Keep the lamp close. When you feel it watching — do not run. Running is what it wants.
